# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

A make.fun program/v1 entry for competition
`u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a`.

## What it does

`main.py` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`: a one-sentence
pitch for a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd at first glance but
turns out to be a real business on a second look.

The pitch: **professional mourners for hire** — trained criers who weep at
strangers' funerals, because grieving families pay $300 a head to make a
small service look deeply loved. Hired mourning is a real, centuries-old
profession (China, the Middle East, parts of Africa, Victorian England),
which is exactly why the joke lands and then pencils out: the odd thing
being sold is named, the buyer is named, and the reason they pay is in the
sentence itself.

## Running it

```sh
MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 -P -s main.py
```

Deterministic, no network, no environment secrets, exits 0. The output
line is 160 characters — inside the 1–200 character gate.
